# AI Prompts for UGC Advertisement Creation

## Prompt 1: UGC Advertisement Script

Act as a professional UGC advertisement scriptwriter.

Create a 30-second Instagram Reels advertisement for an everyday handbag designed for college students.

Include:
1. An attention-grabbing opening hook.
2. A relatable customer problem.
3. A product introduction.
4. Three product benefits.
5. A natural product recommendation.
6. A clear call to action.

Use simple, conversational English.

Present the result in a table containing the scene, dialogue, visual direction, and duration.

Do not invent unverified product claims.

## Prompt 2: Advertising Hooks

Generate 10 different opening hooks for the handbag advertisement.

Include curiosity-based, problem-based, relatable, and benefit-focused hooks.

Keep each hook short and suitable for Instagram Reels.

## Prompt 3: Social Media Captions

Generate five Instagram captions for the handbag.

Include a short product description, a suitable call to action, and relevant hashtags.

Use a friendly and engaging tone.

## Prompt 4: Platform Adaptation

Adapt the handbag advertisement for:
- Instagram Reels
- Facebook posts
- YouTube Shorts

Adjust the format, length, and tone for each platform.

## Prompt 5: Content Improvement

Review the generated advertisement.

Check whether the hook attracts attention, the benefits are clear, the language sounds natural, and the call to action is effective.

Suggest improvements without inventing product features.
